# Data

Maya trains and evaluates on **non-clinical hospital operations** only. No
diagnosis, treatment, prescription, triage, autonomous clinical decision-making,
or identifiable patient records are used.

## Primary corpus: deterministic synthetic operations data

The core training/evaluation corpus is generated by `maya.data.generate_synthetic_dataset`
and is **deterministic by construction**: the same program emits both the prompt
and the exact ground-truth answer, so arithmetic and structured operational
calculations are correct without an external labeler. This keeps the dataset
small, verified, and leakage-controlled rather than large and unverified.

Generated data is written to `data/processed/` (git-ignored) as `train.jsonl`,
`validation.jsonl` and `test.jsonl`, plus a `dataset_manifest.json` recording
audit results and scenario-level split assignments.

## Candidate public datasets (documented, not yet ingested)

Real public datasets that could serve as reference/grounding material for the
retrieval experiments (E2/E3) are listed in `source_manifest.example.json`.
Highlights and license notes:

| Dataset | Repository | Relevance | License | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hospital Dashboard | `HHS-Official/hospital-dashboard` | capacity/reporting rates | public (HHS) | aggregate, non-clinical |
| COVID-19 capacity | `HHS-Official/covid-19-reported-patient-impact-and-hospital-capa` | bed occupancy | public (HHS) | discontinued May 2024 |
| Medicare outpatient | `HHS-Official/medicare-outpatient-hospitals-by-geography-and-ser` | billing/service | public (CMS) | billing administration |
| Hospital prices | `kevykibbz/hospitals` | billing | CMS public | pricing transparency |
| Budget reasoning | `NexusAI123/hospital-financial-reasoning` | finance | MIT | 200+ examples |
| Hospital master | `japan-opendata/japan-healthcare-hospital-master` | facility registry | CC-BY-4.0 | non-clinical master data |

**Excluded** (clinical scope, outside Maya's boundary): MIMIC-III/IV, eICU,
mortality-prediction datasets, antibiotics-usage studies, readmission data,
chest X-ray collections, RSV/COVID hospitalization surveillance, and any
AI-generated "patient outcome" datasets such as
`infinite-dataset-hub/HospitalAdmissions`.

> Public visibility does not imply permission to train. Any dataset whose
> intended use is incompatible with its license is excluded unless written
> permission is obtained. The manifest records license, processing operations,
> hashes and row counts before any ingestion.

## Leakage controls

- Splits are made at the **scenario** level, not the row level, so variations,
  paraphrases and derived copies of one scenario stay in one partition.
- The final `test` split is locked and never used for hyperparameter tuning or
  checkpoint selection.
- Exact-duplicate removal uses a SHA-256 hash of the normalized text; near-
  duplicate detection uses deterministic MinHash + Jaccard.
